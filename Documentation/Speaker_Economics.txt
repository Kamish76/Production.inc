# Speaker Production Economics Analysis

## SPEAKER ECONOMICS (Plastic Enclosure - Simplified)
### Production Costs:
- Wires (2x): 2 × $12 = $24
- Circuits (1x): 1 × $25 = $25  
- Sound Driver (1x): 1 × $45 = $45
- Plastic Enclosure (1x): 1 × $18 = $18
**Total Component Cost: $112**

### Raw Material Costs (to make components):
- Basic Metals: (2×2 + 1×3 + 1×1) = 8 units × $3 = $24
- Advanced Metals: (1×2) = 2 units × $8 = $16  
- Plastic: (2×1 + 1×2 + 1×4) = 7 units × $2 = $14
**Total Raw Material Cost: $54**

### Profitability:
- **Sell Price**: $80
- **Component Cost**: $112 (if buying components) 
- **Raw Material Cost**: $54 (if making everything)
- **Profit if making components**: $80 - $54 = $26 profit (48% margin)
- **Loss if buying components**: $80 - $112 = -$32 loss

## STRATEGIC INSIGHTS:
1. **Vertical Integration Required**: Must make your own components to be profitable
2. **Plastic Focus**: Simplified to plastic enclosure only - no wood complications  
3. **Early Game Strategy**: Players sell basic parts while building speaker capability
4. **Resource Bottleneck**: Advanced metals are the most expensive constraint
5. **Production Investment**: 25 second production time but $26 profit potential
6. **Simplified Production**: No choice paralysis - one clear speaker product path
