# Sprint 4: Version 2.0 (v2.0 Major Update) — Phases 1 through 7

> **Timeline**: September 23, 2026 – Present  
> **Milestone**: Version 2.0 Major Update  
> **Status**: Core Phases 1–5 Completed; Phases 6 & 7 in Active Finalization

---

## 🎯 Overview
**Version 2.0 (v2.0)** marks the monumental transformation of **Production.Inc** from a single-loop idle workshop into a multi-layered industrial tycoon simulation. The update spans seven comprehensive phases (**Phases 1 through 7**), introducing deep progression gating, B2B commercial logistics, branching industrial tech trees, idle research systems, dynamic company prestige, ergonomic UI refinements, and scalable automation overhauls.

---
